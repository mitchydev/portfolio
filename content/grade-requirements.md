# Grade Requirements

## Overall Letter Grades

Letter grades for the course are determined by the quality of your portfolio and whether it demonstrates mastery of the core competencies. We use a simple tier system to determine your letter grades as follows:

| Tier | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| C | All core competencies & one role’s advanced competency mastered |
| B | C + one additional role’s advanced competency mastered |
| A | B + one additional role’s advanced competency & quality competency mastered |

D: At least 50% of the core competencies are mastered.

F: Less than 50% of the core competencies are mastered.

Subsequently, there are no plus/minus grade distinctions in the course. You have either satisfied or not satisfied the requirements listed above.

Competency mastery is measured on a Satisfactory/Non-satisfactory basis on whether the relevant artifacts presented in your portfolio demonstrate adequate mastery of that competency. In your 1-on-1 meetings, you will receive specific guidance on whether the artifacts you’ve gathered thus far constitutes an S versus N grade and what you need to do to improve your portfolio.
