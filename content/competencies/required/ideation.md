### Competency Demonstration

To discover solutions to users' needs, I initially used a combination of observation and interviewing. From there, I used a persona exercise to brainstorm from the perspective of a super busy student who just wants to find a room fast without wasting time. I also applied the SCAMPER method to think about better ways to develop the app, which directly led to cutting out unnecessary tracking to streamline the design.

From this ideation phase, I realized our absolute must-have is a frictionless data-entry system, since our biggest hurdle is relying on users to provide accurate availability. We need to make it incredibly easy for students who don't even have the app to just scan a QR code on the door. Once scanned, it will ask how long they plan to use the space and instantly update the database, followed by a prompt to sign up for the app. For our active users, the app must immediately open to a list view rather than our originally planned map view to keep taps to an absolute minimum. Students want a tool that makes their lives easier, not an extra chore on top of their packed schedules. Finally, we must include a "favorite rooms" feature, while permanently dropping the gym and dining hall heatmaps, since students already know those predictable busy times and are bound by their own daily schedules anyway.
